ScholarED helps schools turn assessment data, gradebooks, and academic reports into student support signals, leadership briefs, and follow-up workflows, so school teams can act before late-stage review points.

Built for school owners, heads of school, and academic directors. Works with IB, GCSE, SAT/ACT, and more.

### The academic review gap

Schools already collect assessment results, gradebook exports, benchmark data, and meeting notes. The problem isn't a lack of data. It's a lack of review structure.

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <b>Risk discovered too late</b><br>
      By the time mock exams or external assessments confirm a decline, the intervention window may already be narrow.
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <b>Fragmented evidence</b><br>
      Subject-level patterns are hard to connect across spreadsheets, reports, and review meetings.
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <b>Untracked follow-up</b><br>
      Support actions are discussed in meetings but not always tracked across evidence cycles, so it's hard to know what has been tried.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

### How it works

Start with one gradebook export. No system integrations required.

| Step | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| **01&nbsp;·&nbsp;Upload&nbsp;evidence** | Upload a gradebook or academic report export. |
| **02&nbsp;·&nbsp;Review&nbsp;patterns** | Academic evidence suggests where support may be needed, and patterns are surfaced for staff review before any action is taken. |
| **03&nbsp;·&nbsp;Assign&nbsp;support** | Record agreed follow-up steps for specific students or cohorts, and route ownership to the right department or staff member. |
| **04&nbsp;·&nbsp;Track&nbsp;progress** | Upload the next round of evidence to review academic movement and keep a record of the support provided. |

### What we believe

- **Evidence before urgency.** Support decisions are stronger when they start from structured academic evidence, not from the most recent incident.
- **Human review before decisions.** ScholarED surfaces patterns for staff consideration. Qualified educators review and act. The system does not decide.
- **Workflow before dashboards.** Schools need a coordinated process, not another chart. Evidence is only useful when it leads to a recorded action.

### What ScholarED is not

ScholarED isn't an LMS, gradebook, curriculum platform, chatbot, or autonomous decision system. It works alongside the systems your school already uses, as a coordination layer for academic review and intervention.
