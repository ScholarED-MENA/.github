# Security policy

Schools trust ScholarED with academic information about their students. We take the security of our products seriously and appreciate reports that help us keep that information safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **[support@scholared.education](mailto:support@scholared.education)** with the subject line **"Security report"**.

Please don't report security issues through public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests.

Include as much of the following as you can:

- a description of the issue and its potential impact;
- the affected product, page, or URL;
- steps to reproduce the issue, or a proof of concept;
- how we can contact you for follow-up.

We'll acknowledge your report, investigate, and keep you informed until the issue is resolved. Please give us reasonable time to address the issue before disclosing it publicly.

## Research guidelines

When investigating a potential issue, please:

- only test with accounts and data that you own or have explicit permission to use;
- stop and report as soon as you encounter student, staff, or school data, and don't access, change, download, or keep it beyond the minimum needed to demonstrate the issue;
- describe any personal information you encountered instead of including it in your report;
- avoid anything that could disrupt service for schools, such as denial-of-service testing, spam, or social engineering of our staff or school users.

## Scope

- The ScholarED website at [scholared.education](https://scholared.education)
- ScholarED applications and services

We don't currently offer a paid bug bounty.
