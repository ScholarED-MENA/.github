# Getting help

- **Schools interested in ScholarED:** [request a demo](https://scholared.education/#demo) or [discuss a school pilot](https://scholared.education/school-pilot).
- **Schools using ScholarED:** contact your ScholarED representative or email [support@scholared.education](mailto:support@scholared.education).
- **Security issues:** follow our [security policy](https://github.com/ScholarED-MENA/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md). Please don't report them publicly.
- **Anything else:** email [support@scholared.education](mailto:support@scholared.education).

Please don't send student names, IDs, grades, or other personal information by email or in screenshots unless we have agreed on a secure way to share them.
